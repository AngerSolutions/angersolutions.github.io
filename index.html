Our anger management program is designed to help clients recognize, overcome and control anger. It's a cognitive behavioral program which includes conflict resolution, social skill building and commitment to change.

Clients complete homework, each week, prior to attending an online group session. During group time, clients present their homework to the group. The facilitator either passes the client to the next module or directs them to redo the homework based on objective criteria. The groups are open-ended so new clients can enter at any time after receiving their workbook and completing the onboarding session.
