The anger management program, Coping with Anger, is designed to help clients recognize, overcome, and control anger. The program is appropriate for both youth and adults. This cognitive behavioral program includes conflict resolution, pro-social skill building, and commitment to change.

The anger management workbook has eight modules, which can be completed in 8 to 10 weekly group sessions. Clients complete homework for each module prior to coming to the group session. During group time, clients present their homework to the group, and the facilitator then passes the client to the next module — or the facilitator directs the client to redo the homework based on objective criteria. The anger management groups are open-ended, meaning that new clients can enter an ongoing group at any time. Each group session usually has new clients as well as some finishing the program. The workbook is individualized to specific client needs by requirements to meet with the facilitator at several modules.
